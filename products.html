Anatomy of a PuzzleLab box

The same four standards apply to every product on this page, whether it costs fifteen dollars or eighty.

  • 2.2mm blueboard core

    Recycled Dutch blueboard with a matte linen top sheet. Pieces hold their shape after hundreds of solves and never delaminate at the tabs.

  • Steel-rule random cut

    No repeated piece shapes across a single puzzle, so a near-fit is never a false positive — every click is the right one.

  • Soy-based, low-glare inks

    Colour-matched to the original artwork and finished with an anti-glare coat, so long evening sessions stay easy on the eyes.

  • Missing-piece guarantee

    Every box is counted by weight and by hand. If a piece is ever missing, we replace the whole puzzle — no receipt hunting.
