Before you upload

What makes a great photo puzzle

A puzzle is only as good as the picture it’s cut from. Here is what our print team looks for — and what quietly ruins a build.

Works beautifully

  • 3000 px on the long edge

    Roughly a 6-megapixel photo. Anything above this prints crisply at full 50 × 70 cm size.

  • Varied colour and texture

    Different regions give solvers footholds. Portraits, landscapes and busy street scenes all cut well.

  • Even, natural light

    Daylight shots keep skin tones and greens accurate once they hit our soy-based inks.

Causes trouble

  • Screenshots and app saves

    Messaging apps compress heavily — fine on a phone, but soft and mushy at poster size.

  • Large flat areas

    An empty sky turns into dozens of identical pieces. Charming for experts, miserable for gifts.

  • Text near the edges

    We trim 3mm on every side, so keep names and dates well inside the frame of your photo.
