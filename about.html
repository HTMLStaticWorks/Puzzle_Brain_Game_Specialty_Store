Our story

It started with one missing piece

In 2014 our founder spent three weeks on a 1500-piece puzzle, only to find the last piece missing. The manufacturer offered a coupon. We thought puzzlers deserved better.

So we opened a tiny shop in Mindville stocking only the puzzles we could personally vouch for — thick board, honest cuts, mechanisms that click. Twelve years on we ship worldwide, but the rule hasn’t changed: if our team wouldn’t keep it, we don’t sell it.

Our passion for puzzles

We are puzzlers first, shopkeepers second

Our team includes a competitive speed-puzzler, a retired cabinet maker and two former maths teachers. Between us we finish around 300 puzzles a year.

  • We time every solve

    The completion times on our product pages are our own — averaged across at least three team members.

  • We work with makers we know

    Most of our mechanical puzzles come from small workshops we have visited in person.

Why we curate our games

Fewer puzzles, chosen better

Big stores stock thousands of titles. We stock around 120, because each one has passed four tests.

Solved in-house

Every title is completed end to end before it is listed. If the last hundred pieces are a chore, it goes.

Honestly rated

Difficulty and solve time come from our own clocks, not the box — so you can compare like for like.

Built to last

Thick board, hardwood and machined metal. Puzzles you can solve again, lend out and pass down.

Actually fun

Hard is fine. Unfair isn’t. We cut anything that relies on tricks, missing info or luck.

Benefits of brain games

Good for the mind, at every age

Puzzles are more than a pastime. Regular, screen-free problem solving supports real everyday skills.

Spatial reasoning

Rotating shapes in your head is the core skill behind 3D and mechanical puzzles.

Logic & problem solving

Puzzle boxes and teasers reward testing a theory, failing and trying again.

Focus & patience

A long jigsaw builds sustained attention, one small win at a time.

Fine motor skills

Chunky kids puzzles develop the grip and coordination used for writing.

Stress relief

Tactile, screen-free play is a calm, meditative break from a busy day.

Time together

A puzzle on the table gets the whole family talking — no screens required.

Quality & safety

Safe for small hands, tough enough for big ones

  • Kids range tested to EN 71 and ASTM F963 toy-safety standards
  • Non-toxic, water-based paints and soy-based inks
  • FSC-certified wood and recycled 2.2mm blueboard
  • Every jigsaw counted by weight and by hand before boxing
  • Clear age labels and small-parts warnings on every product
